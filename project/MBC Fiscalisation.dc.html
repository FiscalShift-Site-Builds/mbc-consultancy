How we run it

01 We check the fit A short review of how you invoice today, your VAT position and which system holds your sales data.
02 Sign-up and configuration We register your business on the platform, set up tax rates and connect it to ZIMRA’s FDMS.
03 Go live Fiscal invoices are issued from your existing system, transmitted in real time, typically within 48 hours of sign-up.
04 Ongoing reconciliation Each period we reconcile your VAT return against what the platform actually transmitted to ZIMRA.
