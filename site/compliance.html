Authority by authority

NSSA

National Social Security Authority

Registration, contributions, compliance reporting and exemption applications. We also correct historic filings where monthly schedules were missed.

ZIMRA

Zimbabwe Revenue Authority

Tax compliance, PAYE, VAT, corporate tax, customs and excise, and dispute resolution. As a licenced tax agent we handle TaRMS and tax head registrations, income tax returns, QPDs and tax clearance certificates.

CIPZ

Companies and Intellectual Property of Zimbabwe

Company registrations and re-registration, CIPZ e-papers, changes of directors and secretaries, and annual returns. Section 303(9) of the Companies and Other Business Entities Act requires all companies to re-register; failure to do so results in de-registration.

PRAZ

Procurement Regulatory Authority of Zimbabwe

PRAZ registration, a requirement for doing business with public entities. Get registered, get paid.

Vendor

Ministry of Finance registration

Assistance with registration on supplier databases, tender portals and government procurement platforms, including the vendor number that identifies approved government suppliers.

Also handled: business licencing, trust registration, tax clearance and company secretarial filings.
